# Personal Wellness Plan (7 Days)

## Overview
A simple, 30-min/day wellness plan to support **safe weight loss and better energy** with **vegetarian meals**, **minimal equipment workouts**, and **back-friendly movement**. Aim for a **10:30pm bedtime**, steady hydration, and consistency over intensity.

## Physical Plan
### Weekly movement schedule (≤30 min/day)
**Back-friendly focus:** Move with control, keep a neutral spine, and stop if pain increases (mild muscle effort is OK; sharp pain is not).

- **Day 1 (Mon) — Walk + Core/Back Care (30 min)**
  - 20 min brisk walk
  - 10 min mobility/stability: **Cat-Cow + Bird Dog** (slow, controlled)

- **Day 2 (Tue) — Bodyweight Strength (15–25 min)**
  - 3 rounds (rest as needed):  
    - Squats (8–12)  
    - Incline or knee push-ups (6–10)  
    - Plank (15–30 sec)  
  - Optional: 5 min easy walk cooldown

- **Day 3 (Wed) — Gentle Yoga or Mobility (20–30 min)**
  - Choose a gentle flow emphasizing hips/hamstrings/thoracic spine (avoid deep forward folds if they irritate your back)

- **Day 4 (Thu) — Walk + Core/Back Care (30 min)**
  - 20 min brisk walk
  - 10 min: **Cat-Cow + Bird Dog**

- **Day 5 (Fri) — Bodyweight Strength (15–25 min)**
  - Repeat Day 2 (focus on clean form, not speed)

- **Day 6 (Sat) — Fun Cardio (30 min)**
  - Recreational activity: swimming, cycling, or a light hike (keep it conversational)

- **Day 7 (Sun) — Rest / Gentle Recovery (10–20 min optional)**
  - Easy walk or light stretching if it helps you feel better

## Mental Plan
### Sleep plan (aim: lights out by 10:30pm)
**Core rules (daily):**
- Keep bedroom **cool, dark, and quiet**
- Keep wake/bed times consistent
- **No screens after 9:30pm** (supports 10:30pm bedtime)
- Avoid caffeine after **2:00pm** and avoid heavy meals close to bedtime

**Nightly routine (choose the day’s focus):**
- **Day 1:** Gentle stretching + reading  
- **Day 2:** Warm bath + 10 min meditation  
- **Day 3:** Light yoga + calming music  
- **Day 4:** 15 min journaling (brain-dump + 1 win from the day)  
- **Day 5:** Read a light book (30 min)  
- **Day 6:** Gentle stretching + warm herbal tea  
- **Day 7:** Weekly reflection + plan next week (simple to-do list + meal/workout glance)

### Stress & energy supports (daily, 5–10 minutes)
- **Mindfulness/meditation:** 5–10 min daily (or after your walk/workout)
- **Breathing reset:** 5 slow minutes when stressed (inhale 4, exhale 6)
- **Media limits:** Set a daily cap on news/social scrolling
- **Time boundaries:** Pick top 1–3 priorities for the day; defer the rest
- **Connection (2× this week):** Call/text a friend or family member for a short check-in

## Tracking (simple checklist)
Use one line per day.

- [ ] Moved (≤30 min)  
- [ ] Did back-friendly mobility/core (Cat-Cow/Bird Dog or gentle yoga)  
- [ ] Ate 3 balanced vegetarian meals  
- [ ] Protein included at least 2 meals  
- [ ] Hydration check (water through the day)  
- [ ] No caffeine after 2pm  
- [ ] No screens after 9:30pm  
- [ ] In bed by 10:30pm  
- [ ] 5–10 min mindfulness or breathing  
- [ ] Stress level noted (1–10): ___  

## Disclaimer
This plan is for general wellness and is not medical advice. With **lower back discomfort**, consult a healthcare professional (or physical therapist) before starting or changing exercise. Stop any movement that increases pain, numbness, or radiating symptoms, and seek medical guidance.
