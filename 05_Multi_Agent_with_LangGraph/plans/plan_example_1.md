# Personal Wellness Plan (7 Days)
## Overview
A simple, light 7-day plan to improve sleep and reduce stress while keeping routines short, honoring a **6:30 AM wake time**, avoiding **caffeine after 12 PM**, and working around **high work-related screen time**. The focus is on light movement, steady meals, and a consistent evening wind-down to support calmer days and better nights.

## Physical Plan
### Daily anchors (every day)
- **Morning (5–10 min):** light stretching or gentle yoga (especially neck/shoulders/back).
- **Midday (5–15 min):** short walk break if possible (even indoors or around the block).
- **Hydration:** drink water throughout the day (especially around activity).
- **Nutrition guidelines:**
  - **No caffeine after 12 PM.**
  - Aim for **light, balanced meals** (whole grains, lean protein, fruits/vegetables).
  - **Evening:** calming herbal tea (e.g., chamomile).
  - If hungry before bed: **small snack** (banana or handful of nuts).
- **Sample meal structure (use any day):**
  - Breakfast: oatmeal + berries + honey  
  - Lunch: quinoa salad + mixed greens + grilled chicken  
  - Snack: Greek yogurt + cinnamon  
  - Dinner: baked salmon + broccoli + sweet potato  
  - Evening: herbal tea (optional small dark chocolate)

### Exercise schedule (7 days)
- **Monday:** 20-min walk + 10-min gentle stretching (neck/shoulders/back)
- **Tuesday:** 15-min bodyweight (squats, wall push-ups, plank) + 5-min deep breathing
- **Wednesday:** Rest **or** 20-min gentle yoga (relaxation-focused)
- **Thursday:** 20-min walk + 10-min stretching **with progressive muscle relaxation**
- **Friday:** 15-min bodyweight (lunges, wall push-ups, seated leg lifts) + grounding (5-4-3-2-1 senses)
- **Saturday:** 30-min recreational light activity (swim/cycle/hike; aim for nature if possible)
- **Sunday:** Rest **or** 15-min light stretching + short meditation

### Physical safety & sleep support
- Stop if you feel pain or dizziness; modify intensity as needed.
- Keep activity **light** and earlier in the day if evening exercise disrupts sleep.

## Mental Plan
### Sleep routine (every day)
- **6:30 AM:** wake time (consistent, including weekends).
- **8:30 PM Wind-down (about 45–60 min):**
  - Dim lights; shift to calm activities.
  - **Reduce screens as much as possible** (aim for 1 hour pre-bed when feasible; if not, minimize stimulation/brightness).
  - Light stretching or gentle yoga (5–10 min).
  - Warm shower or bath.
  - Paper book reading or journaling (20–30 min).
  - Relaxation practice: deep breathing (slow, steady breaths).
- **9:30 PM:** in bed, ready to sleep.
- **Sleep environment:** cool, dark, quiet.

### Stress reduction (daily practices)
- **Morning (2–5 min):** brief mindfulness or journaling to set 1 intention for the day.
- **Midday reset (5–10 min):** short walk break (preferably outside).
- **Afternoon (5 min):** box breathing (inhale 4, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4).
- **Evening (2–3 min):** gratitude/reflection (3 good moments from the day).
- **Information diet:** limit news and social media (especially in the evening).

## Tracking (simple checklist)
- [ ] Wake at **6:30 AM**
- [ ] No caffeine after **12 PM**
- [ ] Light movement completed (walk/yoga/stretch/bodyweight)
- [ ] Midday break taken (5–10 min)
- [ ] Afternoon breathing (5 min)
- [ ] Evening wind-down started at **8:30 PM**
- [ ] In bed by **9:30 PM**
- [ ] Screen time reduced in the last hour before bed (or minimized/low-stimulation)
- [ ] Herbal tea/evening calm routine done
- [ ] Gratitude/reflection completed

## Disclaimer
This plan is for general wellness support and is not medical advice. Adjust based on your body and circumstances. If stress, anxiety, or sleep problems persist or worsen, consider consulting a qualified healthcare or mental health professional.
