## Stress Reduction Techniques
- **Deep breathing**: Inhale 4 counts, hold 4, exhale 4.
- **Progressive muscle relaxation**: Tense/release muscle groups from toes to head.
- **Grounding (5-4-3-2-1)**: 5 see, 4 hear, 3 feel, 2 smell, 1 taste.
- **Nature walks**: Spend time outdoors to reduce stress.
- **Mindfulness practice**: 5–10 minutes daily (meditation or mindful breathing).
