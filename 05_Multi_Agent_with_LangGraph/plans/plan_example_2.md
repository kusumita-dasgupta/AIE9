# Personal Wellness Plan (7 Days)

## Overview
A beginner-friendly, low-impact 7-day plan to **build strength and support weight loss** in **20–30 minutes/day**, designed for **knee-sensitive joints**, **home-based routines**, and a **mostly vegetarian, simple-prep** eating style. Keep intensity moderate, focus on consistency, and prioritize good form and recovery.

## Physical Plan

### Movement (20–30 min/day, low impact; knee-friendly)
**Day 1 (Mon) — Walk + Mobility**
- 20 min brisk walk (flat route)
- 10 min gentle stretching (legs + back)

**Day 2 (Tue) — Beginner Strength (Bodyweight)**
- 15–20 min circuit (move slowly, pain-free range):
  - Wall push-ups (5 min total work)
  - Chair squats *only as tolerated* (or replace with sit-to-stand from a higher chair) (5 min)
  - Planks: 10–15 sec hold, rest, repeat (5 min)
- Optional: 5 min easy stretching

**Day 3 (Wed) — Recovery**
- Rest **or** 20–30 min gentle yoga (flexibility + relaxation)

**Day 4 (Thu) — Walk + Mobility**
- 20 min brisk walk
- 10 min gentle stretching

**Day 5 (Fri) — Strength (Knee-Friendly Lower Body + Upper Body)**
- 15–20 min circuit:
  - Seated leg lifts (5 min)
  - Modified push-ups (knees or incline on couch) (5 min)
  - Side leg raises (5 min)
- Optional: 5 min stretching

**Day 6 (Sat) — Low-Impact Cardio**
- 30 min recreational activity (choose one): cycling, swimming, easy hiking/walking on even terrain

**Day 7 (Sun) — Rest**
- Rest day (optional: easy walk or light stretching if it feels good)

### Physical safety & form notes
- Keep all movements **low impact** and **pain-free**; stop if you feel sharp knee pain.
- Prioritize form over speed; use a chair/wall support as needed.
- Hydrate daily, especially around walks and workouts.

## Mental Plan

### Sleep focus (one theme per day)
**Day 1:** Set a consistent bedtime/wake time (including weekend).  
**Day 2:** 15-minute wind-down routine (reading or gentle stretching).  
**Day 3:** Optimize room: cool (65–68°F), dark, quiet (mask/blackout if needed).  
**Day 4:** Avoid screens 1–2 hours before bed.  
**Day 5:** No caffeine after 2 PM (swap to herbal tea).  
**Day 6:** Finish exercise at least 3 hours before bedtime when possible.  
**Day 7:** Reflect and adjust: note what improved sleep and what disrupted it.

### Daily stress supports (choose 2–3 per day, 10–20 min total)
- 5–10 min mindfulness meditation (morning breath focus)
- 5 min deep breathing during stress spikes
- 10 min light stretching/yoga for tension relief
- Limit news/social media (set a time cap)
- Brief connection: text/call a friend or family member
- Do a hobby/leisure activity (reading, gardening, etc.)
- End-of-week reflection (Day 7): list stressors + one coping step for each

## Tracking (simple checklist)
- [ ] Moved 20–30 minutes (low impact)  
- [ ] Completed strength or mobility work (as scheduled)  
- [ ] Ate protein with each meal (mostly vegetarian)  
- [ ] Included 2+ servings of vegetables  
- [ ] Hydrated well today  
- [ ] Did 10 minutes of stress practice (any option)  
- [ ] Followed today’s sleep focus  
- [ ] Knee felt okay (no sharp pain); adjusted as needed  

## Disclaimer
This plan is for general wellness support and is not medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional before starting new exercise or nutrition changes—especially with knee sensitivity or any medical conditions. If pain, dizziness, or persistent sleep/stress issues occur, stop and seek professional guidance.
